**egomonk's Core Mission and Business Model**

* egomonk is an innovation and strategic management consultancy founded by Sartaj Anand.
* The organization operates at the intersection of intelligence, attention, and culture to help clients make sense of change.
* Their overarching ambition is to positively impact one billion human lives.
* They advise businesses to proactively cannibalize their own outdated models to capture maximum value over time, rather than waiting for external disruption.
* Their consulting framework is built heavily on Simon Sinek's "Why-What-How" model, prioritizing trust-building and storytelling over purely technical solutions.

**The "Vibe": Techno-Romanticism and Storytelling**

* egomonk's ethos heavily emphasizes the human element within technological advancement, focusing on the relationship between people and technology rather than technology alone.
* This philosophy was showcased during "Techno-Romanticism," an event they co-hosted in Bengaluru with Spacekayak.
* The event's thesis was that "wonder and rigor belong together," pushing back against a purely rationalist approach to technology by placing emotion, nature, and human imagination back at the center.
* At this event, egomonk hosted an installation called "The Builder's Bestiary," which explored the mythology and storytelling that startup founders build around themselves.
* Their overall approach is highly optimistic about technology's potential to usher in abundance, provided that human experience remains the core focus.
